Create Your Quiz

How to use

  1. Select how many questions you want (1, 2, or 3).
  2. Type each question and fill in the 3 answer options.
  3. Select the correct answer by clicking the radio button next to it.
  4. Click Create Quiz — you'll get a shareable link.
  5. Share the link with your players (they'll see a waiting screen).
  6. When everyone is ready, click Start.
  7. Players have 18 seconds to pick an answer (with music!).
  8. After 18 seconds, results are revealed — correct answer highlighted with a vote bar chart.
  9. If you have more questions, click Next Question to continue.
