🤙 How to Use

  1. Select how many questions you want (1, 2, or 3).
  2. Type each question and fill in the 3 answer options.
  3. Select the correct answer by clicking the radio button next to it.
  4. Click Create Quiz — you'll get a shareable link.
  5. Share the link with your surfers (they'll see a surf video waiting screen!).
  6. When everyone is ready, click Drop In.
  7. Players have 18 seconds to pick an answer (with music!).
  8. After 18 seconds, results are revealed with animated vote bars.
  9. If you have more questions, click Next Wave to continue.
